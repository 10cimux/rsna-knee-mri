## Available participant solution write-up

[Multimodal LLM relabeling, OOF self-distillation, and pre-committed noise gates](https://www.kaggle.com/competitions/rsna-knee-abnormality-detection/writeups/multimodal-llm-relabeling-oof-self-distillation)
is indexed as a 77th-place write-up dated 22 October. The author describes jointly
reading reports and image montages, then using out-of-fold visual predictions for
soft-label distillation. They report a strong single ConvNeXt-S and warn that fitting
fusion weights on the 58 Gold studies overfit. These are participant-reported results,
not reproduced here and not an official first-place solution. This supports investigating
supervision quality in future work, not retrospectively validating our failed EXP07.

## What this means for our retrospective

Our small-Gold attention experiment and an ImageNet-initialized model trained on
thousands of derived targets test very different supervision regimes. A weak result
for EXP08 does not disprove attention or ConvNeXt. Our label-quality gates correctly
prevented training with unreliable targets, but we did not solve high-quality large-scale
supervision. We also spent too long iterating near the deadline. Those limitations
belong in the portfolio alongside the engineering successes.

No Discord/private conversations were accessed. No new training, external labels or
winner checkpoints were incorporated during this review.
